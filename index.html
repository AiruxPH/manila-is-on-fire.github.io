WELCOME TO MANILA

A city with a story around every corner.

The City of Manila is the capital of the Philippines, known for its historic neighborhoods, cultural landmarks, markets, and vibrant communities.

Manila is also often used to describe Metro Manila, the wider National Capital Region that includes several other cities. This guide focuses on the City of Manila itself.

THROUGH THE YEARS

A history of resilience.

Manila's identity has been shaped by trade, colonial rule, war, rebuilding, and generations of communities.

Before 1571

A thriving trading settlement

Communities along Manila Bay and the Pasig River maintained connections with neighboring Asian trading networks.

1571

Spanish colonial Manila

Spanish forces established Manila as a colonial capital, and Intramuros developed into its fortified center.

1945

The Battle of Manila

Fighting during World War II devastated much of the city and caused immense civilian suffering.

Today

A city that keeps evolving

Manila continues to blend historic landmarks with active neighborhoods, schools, commerce, and cultural life.
